Paris, the City of Light, is one of the world's most iconic travel destinations. Located in northern France along the River Seine, Paris offers an unmatched blend of art, culture, cuisine, and history.

GETTING AROUND: The Paris Metro is the most efficient way to travel around the city. A single ticket costs about 2 euros. The city is also very walkable, especially the central arrondissements. Consider purchasing a Navigo weekly pass if staying more than 3 days.

TOP ATTRACTIONS: The Eiffel Tower is a must-visit, best experienced at sunset. Book tickets online to avoid long queues. The Louvre Museum houses over 35,000 works of art including the Mona Lisa — plan at least 3-4 hours. Musée d'Orsay features incredible Impressionist art in a stunning converted railway station. Notre-Dame Cathedral, though undergoing restoration, remains an architectural marvel from the outside. The Arc de Triomphe offers panoramic views of the Champs-Élysées.

HIDDEN GEMS: The Canal Saint-Martin neighborhood is perfect for a quiet afternoon stroll away from tourist crowds. Rue Crémieux is the most colorful street in Paris. The Promenade Plantée (inspiration for NYC's High Line) is an elevated park offering unique city views. Place des Vosges in Le Marais is Paris's oldest planned square and far less crowded than major plazas.

FOOD AND DINING: Paris is a food lover's paradise. Start your morning with a fresh croissant and café au lait at any corner boulangerie. For budget dining, try the lunch prix fixe menus — many excellent restaurants offer 2-3 course meals for 15-25 euros. Don't miss: Crêpes from street vendors near Montparnasse, Falafel in the Marais (L'As du Fallafel), Macarons from Ladurée or Pierre Hermé, and French onion soup at a traditional bistro.

BUDGET TIPS: Visit museums on the first Sunday of the month when many are free. Purchase a Paris Museum Pass for 2 or 4 days if planning multiple museum visits. Picnic in parks instead of eating at restaurants — buy baguettes, cheese, and wine from local shops. The Montmartre neighborhood offers amazing views and free entertainment from street artists.

NEIGHBORHOODS TO EXPLORE: Le Marais — trendy shops, galleries, and the best falafel in town. Montmartre — bohemian charm, Sacré-Cœur basilica, and incredible city views. Latin Quarter — student neighborhood with affordable eateries and bookshops. Saint-Germain-des-Prés — upscale cafes, luxury shopping, and literary history.

BEST TIME TO VISIT: Spring (April-June) and Fall (September-October) offer the most pleasant weather. Summer can be hot and crowded. Winter is magical with holiday markets but can be cold and grey. Average daily budget: Budget traveler: 80-120 euros, Mid-range: 150-250 euros, Luxury: 350+ euros.
